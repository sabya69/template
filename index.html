IEI Tripura State Centre

The Institution of Engineers (India), Tripura State Centre was established in 1989. It is the only professional body in the state of Tripura serving the engineering community. The State Centre is located at Pandit Nehru Complex, Gurkhabasti, Agartala.

IEI Tripura State Centre organizes various technical activities including seminars, workshops, training programmes, technical talks, and field visits for the benefit of engineers and technologists in the state.

The State Centre has a dedicated committee representing various engineering disciplines, driving innovation, research, and collaborative development in Tripura.
