About BHAVYA

BHAVYA aims to develop 100 investment-ready plug-and-play industrial parks in and near cities, in partnership with States, Central Public Sector Undertakings and the private sector. The scheme focuses on high-quality infrastructure, proximity to urban centres, multimodal logistics connectivity, and seamless industrial ecosystem development to help investors ground investments with ease.

BHAVYA is designed to strengthen India's manufacturing capacity by providing high-quality industrial infrastructure, seamless multimodal connectivity, logistics access, worker-support facilities, digital infrastructure, and sustainable operations.

VISION

To create a globally competitive industrial ecosystem that enables India to emerge as a leading manufacturing and investment destination.

MISSION

To facilitate development of 100 industrial parks with plug-and-play infrastructure, efficient approvals, multimodal connectivity, and long-term operational sustainability.

A landmark step towards accelerating India's industrial growth! The Union Cabinet has approved Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojna (BHAVYA). This will enable the development of 100 plug-and-play industrial parks, boosting manufacturing, investment and jobs across the nation. The scheme will significantly enhance Ease of Doing Business through streamlined approvals and single-window systems. It will boost logistics and services sectors too.
