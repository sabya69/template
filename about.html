HISTORICAL BACKGROUND

Serving the Engineering Community

The Institution of Engineers (India), Tripura State Centre was established in 1989. Since its inception, it has served as the apex professional body in the state of Tripura dedicated to advancing engineering education, collaborative research, and professional practices.


Operating through various specialist engineering divisions (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Computer Science, and Chemical), the centre hosts technical debates, research seminars, capacity development workshops, and study tours.


Located at Pandit Nehru Complex, Gurkhabasti, Agartala, the State Centre building features excellent learning facilities, an extensive technical library, and executive seminar spaces supporting students and corporate members alike.

OUR VISION

To establish a globally competitive engineering hub in Tripura that fosters sustainable infrastructure, industrial innovation, and technical excellence.

OUR MISSION

To support corporate members, state departments, and academic institutions by delivering professional training, technical talk programs, and certifications.

Committee Members

Executive committee members of the Tripura State Centre representing multiple divisions, managing local policies and engineering sessions.
